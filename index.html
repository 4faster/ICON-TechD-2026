About the Conference

Where Technology
Meets Design

ICON-TechD 2026 is the inaugural International Conference on Technology and Design, organized by the Faculty of Technology and Design at Institut Teknologi dan Bisnis ASIA Malang, Indonesia.

This conference brings together researchers, practitioners, and academicians from three distinct yet interconnected disciplines — Informatics, Computer Systems, and Visual Communication Design — to explore the convergence of computation and creativity.

As a fully virtual conference, ICON-TechD 2026 welcomes participants from around the globe to join online and share cutting-edge research, innovative design practices, and emerging technologies that shape our digital future.

Call for Papers

Scope & Tracks

ICON-TechD 2026 invites lecturers, researchers, and students to submit original research addressing topics within three curated tracks.

Track 1

Informatics & Intelligent Systems

  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Text Mining
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Applications
  • Data Science & Big Data Analytics
  • Web and Mobile Technology

Track 2

Computer Systems & Interactive Technology

  • Internet of Things (IoT) & Embedded Systems
  • Computer Networks & Cyber Security
  • Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) & Multimodal Experience
  • Robotics and Automation Systems

Track 3

Visual Communication & Creative Design

  • Digital Arts, Animation, and Visual Effects
  • UI/UX Design & Spatial Design Trends
  • Branding, Creative Media, and Advertising
  • Cultural & Local Flavor Preservation in Digital Media

Publication Output

All accepted and presented papers will be published in the ICON-TechD 2026 Conference Proceedings (with ISBN). Each accepted paper will be assigned a unique ISBN identifier and included in the official conference proceedings.

Submission Guidelines

Paper Format

Single-column format (ICON-TechD template)

Page Limit

20–24 pages in single-column format (including references), approximately equivalent to 10–12 pages in double-column layout

Language

English only

Originality

Similarity index below 15% without bibliography (checked via Turnitin)

Review

Double-blind, ≥2 reviewers
